## 2. Style guide (give this to the AI image agent with every prompt)

- **Font:** Inter (the panel now uses Inter only). Bold 700 for headlines, 500 for body. No serif, no script, no condensed fonts.
- **Colours:** Primary blue `#2563EB`, deep navy `#0F172A`, accent orange `#F97316` (only for the "ring" or one highlight), background white / `#F8FAFC`, success green `#16A34A`.
- **Look:** clean, flat-to-soft-3D, lots of white space, rounded corners (16–24px), soft shadows. Real Indian small-business people (shop owners, clinic receptionists, sales staff), not stock Americans.
- **Never:** busy gradients, neon, robot/AI clichés, tiny text, fake company logos, fake phone numbers other than `1800 XXX XXXX`.
- **Text in images:** keep it to 1 short line, or leave space and add text yourself in Canva/Figma (AI text often misspells).

**Patterns that work:** missed-call pain hook · "customers call free" · a low price in the ad · free demo/trial · trust numbers · Hindi copy for SMEs.

**Our gap to attack:** Tata says ₹999/month but adds per-minute and per-user costs. We bundle 3,000 minutes, no per-user fee, recording + ring-all included, and show the price upfront. Say "all-inclusive" everywhere.

**Who to target:** clinics/hospitals, manufacturers & dealers, D2C brands, real estate, coaching institutes — businesses already printing a mobile number on ads. Channels: Meta ads (Hindi + English), IndiaMART seller listing ("toll free number service"), Justdial listing, WhatsApp follow-up.
